Ratacombs! (scroll down)

A game in which everyone wants to influence one player, the Vessel, but the game ends if the Vessel figures out who they are.

Ratacombs is a super unique take on social deduction that makes it easier to schedule time to deceive and scheme against your friends. Only 3 players needed, but supports up to 8.

We're still early in our development and are actively looking for a publisher. If you are a publisher or know a cool one, send them our way! If not, we'd still like to keep up with you over on our discord where you can keep up with updates and sign up to playtest the game.

We don't use AI at any point of our production, and never have. We know our users want their entertainment to be escapism, and AI usage takes away from that.
